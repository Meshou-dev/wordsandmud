Questions

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the Shop and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with. Remove Ads and an active VIP subscription come back.

Coins and packs are delivered once, when you buy them, so Apple cannot restore them. If something you paid for never arrived, email us with the order ID from Apple's receipt.

How do I remove ads?

Buy Remove Ads in the Shop. It ends ad breaks and banners for good, and Restore purchases brings it back on a new iPhone. A VIP subscription does the same while it is active.

Rewarded ads stay, but only when you choose to watch one for a prize.

I deleted the app. Can I get my progress back?

No. Your progress, coins and settings are saved only on your iPhone. There are no accounts and we keep no copy, so deleting the app deletes them for good. Remove Ads and VIP can still be restored.

Moving to a new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup normally brings your progress with it.

How do I control ads and tracking?

The app asks once whether it may track you for ads. You can change your answer any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. With tracking off you still see ads, just less tailored to you.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google shows a consent message before any ads so you can choose how your data is used. More in our Privacy Policy.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles every payment in the app, so refunds go through Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I cancel VIP?

Open Settings › your name › Subscriptions, pick Words & Mud and tap Cancel Subscription. VIP stays on until the end of the period you paid for.
