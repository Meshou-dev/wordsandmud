Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 October 2026

This policy explains what happens to your data when you play Words & Mud (كلمات وطيحات) on iPhone.

In short

  • No accounts and no sign-in. We run no server for the game.
  • Your progress, coins and settings stay on your iPhone.
  • Ads come from Google AdMob, which collects device and ad data. Tracking for personalised ads happens only if you allow it.
  • Apple handles all payments. We never see your card or payment details.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

Who we are

Words & Mud is published by Meshari Alzahrani Information Technology Company (شركة مشاري الزهراني لتقنية المعلومات), a limited liability company registered in Saudi Arabia under Commercial Registration No. 7054242719, based in Makkah. We are responsible for the personal data we handle under this policy. Google and Apple are responsible for the data they collect under their own policies.

Data saved on your iPhone

The game saves your progress, levels, stats, coins, items, what you own (such as Remove Ads or VIP) and settings such as language and sound. This data is kept only in the app's storage on your iPhone and is not sent to us. Like other app data, it may be included in your iCloud or computer backups under Apple's terms. Deleting the app deletes it.

Ads by Google AdMob

The app shows ads through Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • Device identifiers. The advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking. Otherwise, identifiers limited to this app or its developer.
  • Your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location.
  • Device and app information, such as model, iOS version, language and app version.
  • Ad data and interactions, such as the ads you see and tap.
  • Diagnostics, such as crash logs and performance data.

Google uses this data to serve ads, limit repeats, measure results, prevent fraud and improve its services. Ads are personalised only if you allow tracking in iOS and, in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, agree in Google's consent message. Otherwise Google shows ads that are not based on tracking you across other companies' apps and websites.

We set the maximum ad content rating to Teen and block categories such as dating and sexual content. We receive only summary ad reports from Google, nothing that identifies you.

Google's use of this data is covered by Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices for ads and tracking

  • Tracking: iOS asks you once. Change your answer any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland: through Google's consent message.
  • Fewer ads: Remove Ads or a VIP subscription ends ad breaks and banners.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, email address, card or other payment details. Apple tells the app what you bought so it can unlock it, and Restore purchases asks Apple for your earlier purchases. If we use a service to verify purchases, it receives only the purchase records Apple provides and a random ID, never payment details. Apple's handling of your data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to answer you, and keep it only as long as needed for that and for our legal obligations.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data or share it for money. Data reaches others only as described above: Google for ads and Apple for purchases. We may disclose information if Saudi law or another applicable law requires it, or to protect our rights and the safety of our players.

Where data is processed

Google and Apple may process data on servers outside Saudi Arabia, including in the United States, under their own safeguards. The email provider we use may also store your messages outside Saudi Arabia.

How long data is kept

  • On your iPhone: until you delete the app.
  • Emails: as long as needed to handle your request and meet our legal obligations, then deleted.
  • Google and Apple: under their own policies.

Children

Words & Mud is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal data, email us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) and other laws that may apply where you live, such as the GDPR, you may have the right to know how your personal data is used, to access, correct or delete it, to get a copy, to object to or restrict some uses, and to withdraw consent.

Most of your data never reaches us. You can delete game data by deleting the app, and turn tracking off in iOS Settings. For anything we hold, such as your emails, write to support@hakimai.app. We may ask for details to confirm the request is yours, and we will answer within the time the law requires. For data Google collects, you can also contact Google through its privacy policy.

You can also complain to the Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA) or to the data protection authority where you live.

Security

We keep as little data as we can. Game data stays on your iPhone, protected by iOS. No storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect what we hold.

Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date. If you keep using the app after a change, the updated policy applies.
